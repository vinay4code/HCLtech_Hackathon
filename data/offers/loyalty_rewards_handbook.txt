## SECTION 1: LONG-TERM LOYALTY APPRECIATION PROGRAM
Campaign Code: OFFER-LOYALTY-CREDIT
Category: LOYALTY
Applicable Segment: LONG_TERM_LOYAL
Eligibility Criteria:
- Minimum Account Tenure: 48 months (4 years) of continuous active telecom service.
- Eligible Contracts: Month-to-month, One year, Two year.
- Eligible Service Types: DSL, Fiber optic, or Landline Phone service.
- Account Status: Active, in good standing.

Benefit Description:
Eligible loyal subscribers receive an exclusive $15.00 monthly bill credit applied directly to their statement for 3 consecutive billing cycles ($45.00 total customer reward value).
Discount Type: MONTHLY_CREDIT
Discount Value: $15.00/month
Promotion Duration: 3 billing cycles.
Validity Period: January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026.

Terms & Conditions:
Non-transferable. Not redeemable for cash. Credit automatically terminates if the account is cancelled prior to completion of the 3-month credit cycle.
